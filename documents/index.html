About the collection

A bureaucratic record of departure

The surviving documents do not form a memoir. They were produced by police, customs, banking, and foreign-exchange offices as the Nazi state restricted Jewish emigration and seized Jewish property. Read together, they reveal both the practical work of leaving—passports, possessions, passage money—and the machinery that followed the family after its departure.

The English pages are editorial facsimiles: the translated text follows the structure of each German original while preserving visible paper, stamps, signatures, damage, and annotations. Select Archival original at any time to compare the source page. Bracketed wording marks editorial clarification.

Principal file: HHStAW 519/3 Nr. 35995, Hessisches Hauptstaatsarchiv Wiesbaden.
